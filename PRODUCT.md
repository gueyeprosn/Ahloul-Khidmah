# Ahloul Khidmah — Product truth

## Purpose
Enable mourides (Sénégal and diaspora) to **join as members** (adhésion + cotisation) or **contribute financially without joining**, then receive a member badge after payment.

## Audience
- Primary: adults joining a cellule / recensement national et international
- Secondary: donors who support projects without becoming adhérents
- Languages: French (default) and Arabic (UI toggle)
- Context: mobile-first (Wave / Orange Money SoftPay), often low-bandwidth

## Primary jobs
1. Complete a light adhesion form → pay Wave or OM → become actif → receive WhatsApp badge
2. Make a one-off contribution → SoftPay → confirmation (not an adherent)

## Constraints
- Do not create an adherent in the database until payment is confirmed
- SoftPay live PayDunya; desktop QR / mobile app deep-link
- Brand identity is fixed: emerald, gold, Amiri + Cairo — not generic SaaS purple/Inter
- Admin dashboard remains separate from public conversion surfaces

## Evidence of success
- Adhérents actifs after completed adhesion payments only
- SoftPay completion rate; badge WhatsApp delivery
- No “en attente / fiche incomplète” ghosts from unpaid forms
